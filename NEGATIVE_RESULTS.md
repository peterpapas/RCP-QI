# Negative results and rejected mechanisms

The [V1–V10 negative-results catalogue](research/archive/RCPQI_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md) records each rejected mechanism, its original motivation, the mathematical or empirical obstruction, supporting evidence, and the evidence required to reopen it. The [experiment registry](research/experiment_registry.md) points to immutable result artifacts.

These are scoped results under stated models and tested settings. They are not universal impossibility theorems for quantum SAT solving.
