# V9 decision brief

**Primary recommendation: D — continue foundational mathematical research.**

The strongest V9 finding is a precise separation: a small perfect-matching CNF has a permanent-valued full generating polynomial but polynomial-time partition-existence decisions. The expanded polynomial is unconditionally too large to print; a compressed arithmetic-circuit lower bound depends on the still-unaccepted VP–VNP claims. Independently, extracting the correct half of a unique marked black-box search space needs `Θ(√N)` quantum queries, which does not rule out structured explicit-input quantum algorithms.

Boolean-equivalent algebraic expressions induce the same phase-oracle action, so raw formal degree/rank cannot by itself lower-bound a SAT verifier or quantum circuit. Three narrow Lean modules verify interpolation/evaluation, phase signs and quotient-query transcript equalities. V1–V8 evidence and negative results remain intact. No new operator, circuit result, general SAT impossibility, or publishable novelty claim is justified.

**Next decision point:** seek a Boolean-invariant measure whose certificate is efficiently obtainable from an explicit structured instance, prove its bound for the *same* classical/quantum model, and compare against algorithms already exploiting that structure. If this cannot be done, assess a carefully scoped limitations synthesis with external novelty review.
