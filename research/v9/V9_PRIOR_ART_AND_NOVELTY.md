The strongest defensible V9 contribution is an **auditable separation of three output models for one explicit CNF family**: expanded generating-polynomial output has `m!` entries unconditionally; an exact compressed arithmetic-DAG lower bound is conditional on an accepted permanent lower bound outside characteristic 2; partition existence is polynomial-time matching. Coupled with Boolean-ideal equivalence and the query-model partition bound, this is a useful limitations framework. It is not yet a credible original standalone impossibility theorem: each main ingredient is standard and the conditional premise is unsettled. A paper would need either a genuinely new restricted-model theorem, a substantially stronger machine-checked formalisation with clear independent value, or an externally reviewed novel synthesis.

No claim is made that a prior-art search proves nonnovelty. Before publication, search the full arithmetic-CSP generating-function and quantum partition literature, obtain independent review of the theorem statements and reductions, and compare with known knowledge-compilation and algebraic-model lower bounds. The repository's `AGENTS.md` separately requires author approval before publishing or public release.
