# V8 algebraic complexity review

## Models and implications

| Model | Object and resource | What the November claims would establish if valid | Transfer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P versus NP | Uniform Boolean decision on bit strings, polynomial Turing time | No direct implication in either direction is proved by the inspected papers | A formal polynomial identity over all field inputs is stronger than agreement on the Boolean cube. |
| VP versus VNP | Nonuniform polynomial families of polynomial arity and degree; size of arithmetic circuits versus exponential Boolean-witness sums of VP verifiers | `VP ⊊ VNP` over fields, with the source-specific characteristic restrictions for the permanent | Arithmetic gates compute formal polynomials; they are not arbitrary Boolean gates or quantum gates. |
| #P and SAT | Number of accepting witnesses versus yes/no existence or witness search | [Valiant's 1979 paper](https://www.cs.bu.edu/faculty/gacs/courses/cs535/papers/Valiant_permanent.pdf) proves 0–1 permanent counting completeness; it does not make an arithmetic circuit bound a uniform #SAT runtime bound | A #SAT count can decide SAT by testing positivity, but a polynomial represented as an exponential sum does not supply a fast evaluator. Search-to-decision for SAT costs polynomially many **decision** calls; the V7 recursive architecture did not cheapen them. |
| P/poly versus uniform P | Polynomial-size Boolean circuit family with independent advice per length versus one uniform algorithm | Neither claimed arithmetic lower bound is a Boolean circuit lower bound | Nonuniform lower bounds can be stronger statements than uniform runtime lower bounds, but the models must match before inferring one. |
| BPP versus BQP | Uniform randomized versus uniform quantum bounded-error time | No conclusion about `NP ⊆ BQP` or `BPP = BQP` | Quantum circuits may use phases, interference and measurement; arithmetic-circuit restrictions do not apply to all such operations. |
| Quantum queries versus gates | Calls to a specified oracle versus full implementation size/depth/qubits | Existing unstructured `Ω(√N)` query bound applies to black-box unique search [BBBV](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9701001); neither VP–VNP manuscript strengthens it | A query lower bound does not automatically cover explicit CNF structure; a low query count does not imply low gate cost. |

The only elementary containment used here is `P ⊆ BQP` by reversible simulation, hence `P=NP` would imply `NP ⊆ BQP`. The converse does not follow. We make **no** inference from the claimed `VP≠VNP` to `P≠NP`, to `NP⊄BQP`, to SAT decision/search lower bounds, or to Boolean circuit lower bounds. The algebraic and Boolean representations have distinct equivalence relations; the Lean-backed counterexample is in `V8_MATHEMATICAL_TRANSFER.md`.

## Established methods checked against primary sources

| Method | Original source and scope | Relevance to the two claimed proofs and RCP-QI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arithmetic circuits, counting | [Valiant (1979)](https://www.cs.bu.edu/faculty/gacs/courses/cs535/papers/Valiant_permanent.pdf); the two pinned source papers define their own circuit models | A sum over assignments is a VNP-style *representation*, not an evaluation algorithm. |
| Polynomial identity testing | [Kabanets–Impagliazzo](https://www2.cs.sfu.ca/~kabanets/papers/poly_derand.pdf) prove conditional hardness–randomness relations for PIT, including a partial converse from permanent hardness | PIT tests whether a circuit is identically zero; it does not compute a partition count or certify existence on Boolean inputs for free. Neither inspected main proof is simply a PIT algorithm. |
| Depth reduction and shifted partial derivatives | [Agrawal–Vinay](https://repository.ias.ac.in/92016/), [Kumar–Saraf](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.5978), and the [shifted-partials limitation paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02103) | Established restricted-model lower-bound methods. Anthropic explicitly uses a balanced-cut decomposition but says it does not follow GCT; OpenAI uses balanced cuts in tensor networks. We do not attribute shifted-partials as the engine of either new claim. |
| Tensor rank and its barriers | [Efremenko–Garg–Oliveira–Wigderson](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09502) | A flattening rank can certify restricted decompositions; general circuit transfer requires more. Anthropic uses tensor spectral prices, not a single flattening rank; OpenAI uses geometry plus a replay alternative. |
| Geometric complexity theory and algebraic natural proofs | [Mulmuley–Sohoni](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/S009753970038715X), [Forbes–Shpilka–Volk](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.05328) | Both are boundary/prior-art context. Anthropic says its proof is outside the GCT representation-obstruction program, although it claims a border consequence. OpenAI discusses determinant orbit closure as a consequence, not as its proof invariant. Neither source establishes a SAT promise detector. |
| Quantum polynomial/adversary methods | [Beals et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9802049), [Høyer–Lee–Špalek](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0611054) | These bound quantum **query** algorithms after defining the input oracle and acceptance polynomial/adversary matrix. An arithmetic VP lower bound is not a substitute. |
| Quantum singular-value transformations | [Gilyén–Su–Low–Wiebe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01838) | QSVT acts on a block-encoded operator. The proof papers do not provide a low-cost block encoding of SAT partition existence or a favorable spectral gap. |

The November papers contain more detailed mathematical claims than this targeted review can certify independently. Their mechanisms are analyzed as **claimed conditional inputs** in `V8_PROOF_TECHNIQUE_ANALYSIS.md`, while model-independent RCP-QI deductions are developed separately.
