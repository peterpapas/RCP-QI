# V8 mathematical transfer and algebraic reconstruction

**Status labels:** the CNF identities below are analytic derivations; the two `z²−z` evaluations are Lean theorems; the unstructured quantum lower bound and hardness facts are established external theorems. Neither November 2026 claimed VP–VNP proof is a premise of the new RCP-QI counterexample.

## A costed algebraic model of CNF and partitions

For a CNF `F=∧_{j=1}^m C_j` on `n` Boolean variables, interpret each positive literal as `x_i` and each negative literal as `1−x_i`. In a field of characteristic zero, define the *formal expression* `c_j(x)=1−∏_{ℓ∈C_j}(1−ℓ(x))` and `q_F(x)=∏_j c_j(x)`. For `x∈{0,1}^n`, `q_F(x)=f_F(x)∈{0,1}`. This circuit has size polynomial in CNF length, although expansion can have exponentially many monomials and individual degrees may grow before multilinear reduction. It is an efficient **representation/evaluator at a given assignment**, not a solver. On the Boolean quotient ring impose `x_i²−x_i=0`; formal polynomial equality before this quotient is stronger than agreement on assignments.

For prefix `p` of length `k`, let `S_p={x∈{0,1}^n:x_1…x_k=p}` and define the integer count `Z_F(p)=Σ_{x∈S_p} f_F(x)`. Then `G_F(p)=1[Z_F(p)>0]`, `Z_F(p)=Z_F(p0)+Z_F(p1)`, and under a unique solution exactly one child has count one whenever the parent has count one. These are identities, not inexpensive ways to obtain the counts. Building a reversible circuit for `q_F(x)` at one assignment is polynomial, while evaluating its **sum over the entire partition** may require exponentially many terms or a new algorithm. Nonuniform `VP≠VNP`, if accepted, concerns symbolic polynomial families over all field values; a fixed instance's count is just a constant and cannot itself inherit that lower bound.

If the count is computed in characteristic `p`, `Z_F(p) mod p=0` need not imply UNSAT. Example: an empty CNF on one free bit has two witnesses and count zero in characteristic two. For a *promised unique solution*, parity and existence agree, but discovering that parity still needs a costed procedure. The existing `RCPQI.clause_quadratic_gadget` shows that moving 3-CNF into degree-two equations over GF(2) leaves general NP-hard satisfiability; degree two alone is no tractability promise.

The phase oracle gives `U_f|x⟩=(-1)^{f(x)}|x⟩`. For uniform `|S_p⟩`, the marked overlap is `Z_F(p)/|S_p|`. On the full uniform state, the zero-frequency amplitude after `H U_f H` is `1−2 Z_F(∅)/2^n`. This is an algebraic encoding of a count, but **reading** a one-witness signal requires repeated coherent or measured use; [BBBV](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9701001) gives `Ω(√(2^n))` verifier queries in unstructured unique search. The circuit `H U_f H` alone has a target-independent measurement distribution in V7's unique-marker calculation. An imagined exact-amplitude readout changes the access model.

## A formal-polynomial transfer counterexample

`P(z)=z²−z` equals zero at both Boolean inputs yet `P(2)=2`. The new Lean module proves these integer-evaluation facts as `boolean_relation_vanishes` and `boolean_relation_nonzero`; it does **not** formalize polynomial rings or circuit complexity. Over any field the corresponding formal polynomial is nonzero (coefficient of `z²` is one), even though it vanishes on `{0,1}`. For any formal representative `Q`, `Q+(z²−z)H` has the same Boolean values for arbitrary `H` but different formal coefficients, degree, derivatives, and tensor flattenings. Thus a lower bound that interrogates formal homogeneous or multilinear components cannot be applied to **every** circuit that computes a Boolean SAT verifier without a theorem invariant under the Boolean ideal. Anthropic's paper explicitly names this failure point in §2.5.

## Direction 1: lower-bound transfer

A valid conditional path would define a restricted model `M` whose output is a **symbolic polynomial family** `C_m(z)` over all field inputs, prove that a known hard VNP family is obtained as `P_m(z)=C_m(φ_m(z))` by a uniform, charged projection, and prove every permitted RCP-QI operation has an arithmetic-circuit simulation. If a size-`s` operator yields a circuit for `P_m` of size at most `h_m(s)`, a known lower bound `L(m)` implies only `h_m(s)≥L(m)`; a direct projection with additive `a(m)` overhead gives `s≥L(m)−a(m)`. This is *not* a bound on Boolean circuits, arbitrary quantum gates, or a single CNF instance. No such projection/simulation is in this repository. A quantum measurement with bounded error and complex amplitudes is especially outside the exact arithmetic model.

The existing V7 known-quotient theorem is a better matched limitation: with a supplied section and fiber-constant verifier, the quotient oracle and original oracle simulate one another in one query. If the residual quotient function is arbitrary, [BBBV](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9701001) still applies to its labels. This statement needs no unverified VP–VNP premise and leaves structured residuals open. **Disposition:** pursue a scoped theorem about oracle models; do not claim a universal SAT or quantum lower bound.

## Direction 2: constructive structural discovery

| Promise | Detection/certification and residual | Matched methods and disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Affine/XOR subsystem `Ax=b` with `d` free variables | Gaussian elimination gives `x=x₀+Bz` in polynomial time; arbitrary nonlinear `H(x₀+Bz)` can still encode 3-SAT, as V7.1 proved by an explicit free-core reduction. | Classical elimination + CDCL and Grover on `2^d` residual candidates. Useful preprocessing control, **no new quantum advantage**. |
| Bounded-width clause/factor hypergraph with supplied decomposition | Check the decomposition; finding a good one and the width cost must be charged. Width `w` supports classical dynamic programming exponential in `w`, polynomial for fixed/bounded-log `w` under standard table assumptions [Allender et al.](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v010a012/v010a012.pdf). | Compare classical width-DP/CDCL to quantum backtracking/walk. A cheap quantum advantage is unproved; **conditional family only**. |
| Low-rank factor/tensor decomposition | Verify supplied factors by a stated method; constructing minimum tensor rank is hard in general [Håstad](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs6241/2019sp/readings/Hastad-1990-rank.pdf). Low rank of a clause incidence matrix need not bound the SAT solution tensor's rank. | Classical factor contraction and SAT preprocessing are first baselines. **Blocked without a certified, efficiently usable factorization of a hard residual.** |
| Polynomial factorization/symmetry | A supplied syntactic factorization is checked cheaply but can be `easy factor × arbitrary hard core`; semantic symmetry certification can encode UNSAT (V7). | Factor-aware CDCL and symmetry breaking already exploit the same promise. **No distinct mechanism established.** |
| Concentrated Walsh spectrum | Must specify a coefficient/sign gap, discover it without solving SAT, and prepare coherent oracle access. A unique marked item has no target-position information in one Walsh outcome (V7). | Classical Fourier/affine solvers, Grover, amplitude estimation. **Inconclusive as a restricted promise, not an architecture.** |

The field-valued tensor prices in the new papers are *lower-bound witnesses* for special symbolic network polynomials; they are not algorithms that discover a SAT instance's useful low-rank structure. A low-width representation that makes exact counting easy classically does not, by itself, create a quantum-specific benefit. [Markov–Shi](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0511069) also shows how small contraction width can make certain quantum circuits classically simulable.

## Direction 3: quantum mechanisms and complete cost

For a proposed operator, charge `P_discover + P_certify + C_compile + R(C_prepare + Q_f C_f + Q_U C_update + Q_reflect C_reflect + C_measure + C_verify)` across all attempts, and separately report logical queries, compiled gates/depth, qubits/ancillas, precision and failure probability. A clause-built diagonal block encoding of `f_F` has singular values 0 and 1; applying [QSVT](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01838) to it does not produce the OR over a partition. The needed marked fraction can be `1/|S_p|`, so extracting a witness with ordinary amplitude amplification costs `Θ(√|S_p|)` verifier uses in the unique black-box case. A tensor-network state could be useful only after proving low-cost preparation/reflection and a favorable signal gap; those are not supplied by an arithmetic lower bound. Quantum walks likewise require a priced transition/check and a proved gap, as in [MNRS](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0608026).

**Gate result:** the algebraic reconstruction is precise and useful for identifying where cost hides. It does not pass Gate 2 for a Boolean or quantum lower-bound transfer, and no candidate passes Gate 3 for a new architecture. The highest-value open theorem is a model-matched characterization: under exactly which certified, cheaply constructible Boolean/tensor promises can a partition statistic be accessed with a gap that beats *both* classical structure-aware solving and known quantum amplification/backtracking?
