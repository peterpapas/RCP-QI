# V5 benchmark family definitions and complexity

The reproducible generator is `src/rcp_qi_research/structural.py`. Seeds `20261126..20261128` produce 33 cases. The verifier and PySAT solver receive clauses only; planted assignments are used solely during generation, and the offline exhaustive audit is used solely for labels. The generator does not certify a random instance as hard.

| Family | Generated instances | Complexity interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XOR-SAT | Six variables in overlapping three-variable parity equations; the generator adds two unit clauses for unique, omits them for four solutions, or adds a contradictory parity equation for zero. Each parity equation is translated to four 3-clauses. | Affine XOR systems admit polynomial-time Gaussian elimination over `GF(2)`. The CNF translation does not turn this control into evidence of general NP hardness. The current benchmark uses PySAT; a dedicated GF(2) baseline remains to be added. |
| 2-SAT | Eight-variable implication chain, with optional unit clauses producing unique, multiple, or zero solutions. | General 2-SAT is decidable in linear time using an implication graph and strongly connected components. This chain is simpler still. PySAT and DPLL are run now; a dedicated SCC control is future work. |
| Planted 3-SAT | Eight variables and 34 random three-literal clauses conditioned to accept one generated assignment. The formula may have additional solutions. | Generation guarantees satisfiability. No worst-case NP-completeness or hardness claim is made for this sampled distribution. The planted assignment is not provided to solvers. |
| Random 3-SAT | Eight variables and 34 independently sampled three-literal clauses, `m/n=4.25`. | Unrestricted 3-SAT is NP-complete, but this finite random distribution is neither an NP-complete promise nor guaranteed hard. The chosen density is a phase-transition-inspired control, not a proof of criticality at `n=8`. |
| Graph coloring | CNF encoding of three-colorability for a triangle (six assignments) and `K4` (zero). One-hot variables encode vertex colors and edge clauses forbid equal adjacent colors. | General 3-colorability is NP-complete ([Garey–Johnson–Stockmeyer, 1976](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0304397576900591)); these two fixed graphs are trivial controls. The encoding uses `|V|×3` Boolean variables, so exhaustive audit cost is measured over encoded assignments. |
| Symmetric CNF | Four independent pairs, each constrained to have opposite Boolean values, giving 16 assignments. Exact signed-variable swaps preserve the clause multiset. | This restricted family is trivial. The symmetry scorer verifies swaps by scanning clauses. It does not perform orbit-quotient search and its score has no demonstrated quantum value. |

The search metrics are wall time, PySAT construction/solve calls and internal decisions/conflicts/propagations/restarts, scorer clause visits/propagation work, and final verification. The V5 run uses no Qiskit circuit, so its quantum oracle queries, gates, depth, state preparations, and measurements are zero/inapplicable rather than silently omitted quantum work. The preceding V1–V4/V6 quantum records remain the matched generic controls at small sizes; a scalable quantum structural comparison is pending a defined operator.
