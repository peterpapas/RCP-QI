# V10 theorem candidate registry

Only **T2 and T3** were selected for bounded deep development. “Lean” means the named Boolean statement, not an external complexity theorem. All candidates use explicit assumptions; no row is a general SAT or quantum impossibility claim.

| ID | Exact candidate, model and evidence | External dependency / prior art | Disposition and novelty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Given a **listed** balanced set of `K` possible addresses and unique-marker membership oracle, output the marked half with bounded error in `Θ(√K)` queries. Certificate/list production charged. | V9 adversary witness on restricted domain and [Grover](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605043); quantum search with advice already studied. | **Stopped:** direct corollary; no explicit-CNF transfer or new mechanism. |
| T2 | Alice privately owns positive unit clauses from `a`; Bob negative units from `b`. Their union is SAT iff `DISJ_n(a,b)`. | Boolean reduction **Lean-verified** (`distributed_units_sat_iff_disjoint`); `Ω(n)` randomized [Razborov 1992](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~razborov/files/dis.pdf), `Ω(√n)` entanglement-assisted quantum [Razborov 2003](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0204025). | **Established restricted distributed-input corollary; stopped.** Direct encoding of known disjointness, not original SAT lower bound. |
| T3 | A full binary branch tree with an original-clause conflict at every leaf refutes `F`; with `T` nodes and max depth `d`, translate to tree-like resolution **with weakening** in `O(Td)` written-literal work. | Lean checks leaf checker and semantic soundness. Analytic translation uses standard [DPLL/tree-like resolution](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~sabhar/jobapps/pubs/sabharwal-pub-cl-jair04.pdf) correspondence; Lean does **not** implement proof translation. | **Established restricted simulation; stopped.** Useful audit of what a discard certificate must contain, not original proof complexity. |
| T4 | On standard pigeonhole CNFs, the T3 restricted architecture inherits resolution proof-size lower bounds after `O(Td)` simulation. | [Haken 1985](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975%2885%2990144-6); scope excludes oracle leaves/CDCL/quantum. | **Known corollary of T3; stopped.** |
| T5 | Exploiting a supplied fiber-constant quotient can be cheap while deciding whether a supplied semantic flip preserves `b∧H(x)` is equivalent to UNSAT of `H`. | V7 `gated_flip_preserving_iff_unsat` Lean theorem; coNP-hardness outside Lean. | **Already established in V7; no fresh candidate.** |
| T6 | A Boolean-invariant measure certified from an explicit CNF yields a new quantum partition-information bound under a charged oracle construction. | No certificate/transfer meeting the model gate; exact/approximate degree and adversary are existing query methods. | **Blocked by missing premise**, not refuted as a universal possibility. |
| T7 | Certified low primal width or affine structure alone forces quantum advantage. | Width DP and residual-3-SAT counterexamples in structured-families note. | **Refuted as stated**; no general negative theorem beyond those controls. |

The two primary developments terminate at direct prior-art reductions. The research does not support an original lower-bound claim, a new circuit, or a limitations-paper submission without an independent novelty assessment identifying a genuinely new theorem.
