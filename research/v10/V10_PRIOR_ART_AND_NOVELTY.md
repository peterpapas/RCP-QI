**Novelty gate outcome:** T2 and T3 terminate as direct corollaries of existing frameworks. The explicit access-model comparisons and Lean certificate checks make a reproducible project record, but no externally credible original theorem beyond textbook/prior-art results was identified. A “limitations paper” could be a useful exposition, yet V10 lacks a defensible *new* mathematical contribution for a research-paper claim. The strongest defensible statement is a negative investment assessment, not a new impossibility theorem. This judgment can change if independent experts identify an overlooked restricted-model theorem; it is not a proof that no such theorem exists.
