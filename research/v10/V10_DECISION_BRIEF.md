# V10 decision brief

**Primary recommendation: D — suspend active RCP-QI development pending a genuinely new mathematical insight.**

V10's two bounded theorem efforts reached known frameworks. Distributed unit-clause SAT is exactly set disjointness, so its strong communication bounds apply only while clause inputs are held by separate parties. A recursive tree that discards branches using checkable original-clause conflicts yields a standard tree-like resolution refutation; known proof lower bounds constrain that restricted classical architecture, not arbitrary quantum computation. Lean verifies both Boolean reductions and the conflict-certificate soundness; it does not formalize the external lower bounds.

No efficiently obtainable Boolean-invariant certificate was found that yields a new explicit-SAT bound or quantum mechanism. High communication rank can coexist with trivially satisfiable bounded-width CNF, and cheap affine structure can leave arbitrary 3-SAT. A focused novelty check found a useful audit trail but no original theorem sufficient to justify a research-paper claim. V1–V9 negative evidence and all experimental artifacts remain preserved.

**Restart gate:** a concrete, independently reviewed theorem proposal must specify an explicit input family, a cheaply verified structural certificate, an information task, a valid classical/quantum model-preserving reduction, and a contribution beyond the cited disjointness, DPLL/resolution and black-box search results. No new circuit is authorised by V10.
