# RCP-QI rejected mechanisms and unresolved controls

**Status:** SUSPENDED — PENDING NEW MATHEMATICAL INSIGHT. “Rejected” below means rejected **under the tested model and assumptions**, not universally impossible. The original [negative-results log](../negative_results.md), [experiment registry](../experiment_registry.md) and immutable raw artifacts remain the detailed record. A proposal must address the listed obstruction before reopening a mechanism.

| ID and original hypothesis | Why it looked useful | Obstruction and evidence | Required new evidence to reopen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N00 Ideal partition-existence oracle gives one decision per bit. | `O(log N)` decisions suggest fast search. | `G(S)=∨_{x∈S}f(x)` hides search unless built and charged; V0 was conceptual only. | Reversible construction with full gate/query cost beating Grover, quantum backtracking and direct SAT. |
| N01 Pair every candidate with one valid/one invalid partner. | One verification could eliminate a pair. | An arbitrary exact pairing needs semantic knowledge; a supplied total complement already yields a witness after one **classical** verifier value (Lean S05). Under unique solution and involution, universe has at most two members (S04). | A nontrivial, efficiently certified **partial** law with a hard residual and quantum-only information gain. |
| N02 Repeated pair elimination preserves complementarity. | Recursive halving seems to compound a pairing gain. | Removing one member can destroy the proposed relation; no invariant or costed partition oracle was proved. | A preservation theorem for every recursion step plus an efficient decision procedure. |
| N03 Deutsch-style or one Walsh sample locates the valid side. | Phase interference can reveal parity/complementarity. | V7 analytic single-sample distribution for one marker is target-independent (A01). | New certified Fourier promise, predicted observable, charged repetitions and matched classical analysis. |
| N04 One standard Grover iteration yields a cheap partition test. | It raises correct-half probability. | `P_half=1/2+4/N−8/N²`; bias is `O(1/N)`, so independent majority sampling needs `Θ(N²)` shots for fixed confidence. V1 formula/statevector agree. | A distinct operator with a nonvanishing certified gap and complete cost below known search. |
| N05 More standard Grover iterations beat Grover by measuring only half. | A coarser answer may need fewer calls. | V2 half probability is algebraically tied to exact-solution probability; fixed accuracy retains `Θ(√N)` generic queries. Saved thresholds at `N=256` take 9/10/11 calls for 90/95/99% one-shot half accuracy. | A structurally different promise/operator, not additional generic Grover tuning. |
| N06 Partition-bit-only reflection or oracle-only phase amplifies the side. | A cheaper reflection might concentrate probability in one half. | V3's `_diffuse(circuit, [n - 1])` reflects on the partition bit only; this and the oracle-only control leave `P_half=1/2` in tested `N=4..64` settings. **Correction:** these results do not test intra-block diffusion. Published Grover–Radhakrishnan/Korepin partial search combines intra-block and global operations and improves block-label query constants, still at `Θ(√N)` generic scaling. [Derivation and new data](../corrections/PARTIAL_SEARCH_PRIOR_ART.md). | A new mechanism needs a distinct structural promise and matched costs beyond established partial search. |
| N07 Dedicated partition ancilla creates new information. | An explicit register may expose phase kickback. | Label-before-data Grover has zero left/right trace distance; label-after only copies existing bias. V3 ideal density matrices. | A certified cross-register interaction that beats equivalent data-register measurement after all costs. |
| N08 Clause-parity phase implements CNF satisfaction. | Local clause phases are easy to compose. | Odd number of violated clauses is not conjunction; V4 counterexample and clause-built oracle control. | A reversible exact CNF marking circuit with charged ancillas/uncomputation and a distinct benefit. |
| N09 Bounded quantum failures prove UNSAT. | No satisfying measurement suggests an empty partition. | Finite failed shots are inconclusive. V4 zero-solution controls were correctly labeled inconclusive; DPLL proved UNSAT. | A sound certificate/proof system for UNSAT or a precise bounded-error decision with its failure probability. |
| N10 Recursive confidence and restarts make the architecture competitive. | Error budgeting may repair bad branch choices. | V6 saved tiny groups include a wrong decision and failed first run; charged restarts raise total calls above Grover. | A pre-registered larger, hard, matched end-to-end advantage including failed runs and verification. |
| N11 Classical adaptive branch scoring is a quantum advantage. | Clause occurrence/connectivity can guide partitions. | V5 is classical PySAT preprocessing; direct solver usually wins. No novel quantum operator was run. | A distinct coherent mechanism whose gain remains after giving the same preprocessing to classical baselines. |
| N12 V5 small-instance overhead disappears at scale. | Harder formulas may amortize scoring. | V5.1 finds limited local prefix gains but no observed direct-CDCL crossover; two seeds, two-second censoring, and timeout counts bound inference. | Independent preregistered hard families, longer matched budgets, credible direct-solver crossover and cost attribution; this alone would still be classical. |
| N13 Symmetry scoring or known quotient yields free quantum speedup. | Fewer orbit representatives reduce domain size. | Known quotient `f=g∘q` transports queries in either direction (Lean S06–S08,S14–S15); classical search uses it too. Semantic certification of a supplied flip encodes UNSAT (S09). | Efficient discovery/certification and reversible representative map plus a quantum-specific operation on the quotient beyond ordinary amplification. |
| N14 Local/constraint complement gives a hard SAT witness. | Flipping one constraint may determine a branch. | Accessible full-validity complement gives one-query classical witness (S10); constraint-only flip converts a **given** residual witness (S11). `b∧H` preserves arbitrary residual SAT difficulty (S12). | A relation that exposes residual information without solving or encoding it in a certificate. |
| N15 Simon/Shor/hidden-shift promises transfer to SAT. | Known quantum Fourier algorithms extract hidden algebraic structure. | Their richer many-output/periodic/shifted oracles are not supplied by a one-bit CNF verifier; construction may encode the answer (V7.1). | A polynomial-cost, solution-preserving explicit-SAT reduction constructing the exact coherent promise oracles. |
| N16 Affine or quadratic syntax alone enables a quantum method. | Algebraic representation seems compressible. | GF(2) elimination is classically polynomial; a free nonlinear residual remains arbitrary 3-SAT (S12). Clause-to-MQ gadget (S13) changes syntax, not hardness. | A certified extra invariant that survives a hard residual and gives a costed quantum gap over Gaussian elimination/CDCL. |
| N17 Low width, low rank or tensor factorization is automatically quantum-specific. | Small interaction graphs support compact representations. | Supplied bounded width gives classical DP; finding/verifying useful decompositions and quantum state preparation must be charged. Width-one equality CNF has high communication rank yet trivial SAT (V10 A11). | An efficiently obtained certificate with a quantum advantage over the same width-aware classical algorithm. |
| N18 New VP≠VNP claims directly lower-bound SAT/quantum circuits. | Claimed formal polynomial bounds are strong. | Independent mathematical acceptance remains unestablished; formal polynomial equality is stricter than Boolean equivalence. V8 `z²−z` counterexample (B01–B02). | Independent source/Lean semantic review **and** a valid arithmetic-to-Boolean/quantum model-preserving reduction. |
| N19 Compact symbolic SAT polynomial gives cheap partition existence. | An implicit expression might encode all witnesses. | V9 perfect-matching CNF produces permanent with `m!` expanded terms, yet every prefix decision is polynomial-time matching (A06–A07). Count evaluation can cancel in finite characteristic. | A theorem about the **decision** output in the exact model, not only full polynomial generation. |
| N20 Raw formal degree/rank lower-bounds Boolean verifier complexity. | Algebraic invariants can certify hardness. | `(z²−z)∏(1+a_i b_i)` is Boolean-zero with raw flattening rank `2^k` and small circuit (A05). | A Boolean-invariant, efficiently certifiable measure and a lower-bound theorem for the intended model. |
| N21 Only the half label evades black-box search cost. | Coarse information may require few oracle calls. | V9 all-ones cross-half adversary gives `Θ(√N)` unique-marker membership queries (A08). | A concrete non-black-box structural promise or richer oracle with complete construction cost. |
| N22 Distributed communication rank lower-bounds central SAT time. | High rank suggests hard information combination. | V10 unit-CNF is set disjointness only under private clause ownership; central union is linearly scanned. Public width-one equality CNF has exponential evaluation rank but easy SAT. | A valid simulation from *every allowed centralized architecture* to a low-communication protocol with preserved ownership/output. |
| N23 Proof-complexity lower bounds cover arbitrary quantum branch elimination. | Discarding a branch resembles an UNSAT proof. | V10 translation requires a complete binary tree with a falsified **original clause** at every leaf (C01–C05,A12). Opaque SAT/quantum decisions and CDCL learning do not satisfy that certificate model. | A sound polynomial-overhead simulation of the stronger architecture by the chosen proof system. |

**Unresolved, not refuted:** a certified structural promise with a hard residual and a distinct quantum information advantage; efficient semantic structure discovery in an appropriate restricted family; explicit-CNF quantum gate/time lower bounds; and independent acceptance of the November 2026 VP–VNP claims. None is an active project workstream. The [restart protocol](RCPQI_RESTART_PROTOCOL.md) is mandatory before revisiting them.
