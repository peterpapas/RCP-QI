# RCP-QI final research report — V1–V10

**Final status: SUSPENDED — PENDING NEW MATHEMATICAL INSIGHT.** Closeout date: 26 November 2026. This is an archive of an unpublished, unproven research programme, not a claim that the original algorithm works. The [executive summary](RCPQI_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md), [theorem catalogue](RCPQI_THEOREM_CATALOGUE.md), [negative-results catalogue](RCPQI_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md), [reproducibility audit](RCPQI_REPRODUCIBILITY.md) and [restart protocol](RCPQI_RESTART_PROTOCOL.md) are the entry points for independent review. Existing Git history, raw data and phase reports are retained.

## 1. Motivation and evolution

The original question was whether quantum interference plus mathematical relations among SAT assignments could eliminate large partitions of an exponentially sized search space at a cost below generic search. The first formulation paired candidates with opposite validity. That law is not available for arbitrary candidates without further structure; when supplied globally with an accessible point, it already yields a witness after one **classical** verifier evaluation. The idea moved to unique-solution complementarity between disjoint exhaustive halves, then to recursive implicit bit-prefix partitions tested by quantum existence procedures. The eventual programme challenged recursion itself and investigated transformations, quotient spaces, structural promises, algebraic representations, communication and proof complexity.

For Boolean verifier `f:{0,1}^n→{0,1}`, partition existence is `G(S)=∨_{x∈S}f(x)`. Under exactly one solution, `G(A)⊕G(B)=1` for disjoint covering halves. This is a mathematical statement about answers, **not** a cheap oracle for those answers. A recursive decision count of `n=log₂N` does not include the cost of each `G`, construction, preparation, measurement, repetition, errors or final verification. The project never established a usable low-cost partition-existence operator for NP-complete SAT.

The [central theorem catalogue](RCPQI_THEOREM_CATALOGUE.md) includes **all 47 named Lean theorems** plus analytic reductions and counterexamples, with exact assumptions, proof locations, complexity scope and novelty status. The Lean statements do **not** formalise Qiskit circuits, Hilbert-space channels, Valiant's theorem, quantum adversary lower bounds, communication lower bounds or Haken's proof bound. Conditional reliability or complementarity hypotheses are not algorithms that discover/certify the hypotheses.

## 4. Established external dependencies and model boundaries

The unstructured query bound [BBBV](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9701001), [Grover's algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9605043), [quantum adversary method](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0611054), [Valiant's VNP completeness](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~billey/colombia/references/valiant.completeness.1979.pdf), [bipartite matching](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0202019), [Razborov's classical/quantum disjointness bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0204025), and [resolution lower bounds](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975%2885%2990144-6) are **cited external results**. Their models are distinct: queries versus gates/time, symbolic polynomial generation versus SAT decision, distributed communication versus centralized SAT, resolution certificates versus arbitrary quantum computation. No invalid transfer is adopted. The newer VP≠VNP proof artifacts are retained as **claims** pending independent semantic review.

## 5. Failed and unresolved hypotheses

The [negative-results catalogue](RCPQI_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md) specifies the promise, obstruction, evidence and required new evidence for each rejected mechanism. Key explicit counterexamples are total complement's immediate classical witness, free-dummy symmetry preserving an arbitrary hard core, target-independent one-sample Walsh outcomes, `z²−z` Boolean/formal divergence, high-rank Boolean-zero `Q_k`, permanent-valued symbolic output with easy perfect-matching decisions, high communication rank with easy equality-CNF satisfiability, and conflict-tree proof bounds that disappear when leaves become opaque oracle verdicts.

Open questions remain about an efficiently certifiable structural promise with a meaningful hard residual and a genuinely quantum-specific information task. The project has **no** proof of `P=NP` or `P≠NP`, **no** novel quantum computational advantage, **no** universal impossibility theorem for quantum SAT solving, and **no** independently established publishable mathematical novelty. A restricted-model negative result is valuable within its stated assumptions but does not settle these open questions.

## 6. Suspension rationale

V1–V6 generic interference/recursion controls found costs and error behavior consistent with known search; V5.1 found no observed direct modern-SAT-solver crossover within its limited protocol. V7–V10 showed that promising algebraic/structural claims either give the same help classically, leave a hard residual, rely on a stronger oracle, or transfer only to a restricted query/communication/proof model. V10's two bounded theorem candidates reduce directly to established disjointness and DPLL/resolution results. Further generic tuning or speculative circuit design has no mathematical gate to pass, and a novelty assessment does not currently support a research-paper claim.

Accordingly the team accepted V10 decision D. **No V11 is authorised or planned.** Active research and quantum architecture development are suspended. The [restart protocol](RCPQI_RESTART_PROTOCOL.md) requires a new, independently reviewed mathematical insight and complete model-preserving cost analysis before any circuit work. This report is a durable handoff, not a request to resume the programme.
