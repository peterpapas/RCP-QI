# RCP-QI closeout — executive summary

**Status: SUSPENDED — PENDING NEW MATHEMATICAL INSIGHT.** The V1–V10 programme investigated whether quantum interference and structural complementarity could cheaply identify which half of a SAT search space contains a solution. It established **no novel quantum computational advantage** and **no general SAT or quantum impossibility theorem**. It proves neither `P=NP` nor `P≠NP`, and independent publishable mathematical novelty was not established.

The strongest validated findings are scoped. One Grover iteration increases correct-half probability to `1/2+4/N−8/N²`, but the bias vanishes as the search space grows. Established [partial quantum search](../corrections/PARTIAL_SEARCH_PRIOR_ART.md), omitted by the original V3 controls, reduces the constant for block identification while retaining `Θ(√N)` generic query scaling. Measured recursive runs and classical adaptive partitions did not beat the tested Grover or direct modern SAT baselines in the saved studies; the larger V5.1 study has limited seeds and two-second budgets. A supplied total complement gives a witness after one classical verifier value; known quotients help classical search too. Algebraic symbolic-output hardness does not imply hard partition decisions: a perfect-matching CNF generates the permanent but each partition decision is polynomial-time matching. V10's communication and proof-system results reduce to established disjointness and tree-like resolution frameworks.

The archive retains all historical raw data, plots, source references and Lean proofs. The final audit verified **38** manifest-indexed artifacts by SHA-256; 65 tests, Ruff, Lean build/axiom audit, result validation and V5.1 integrity checks passed. See the [full report](RCPQI_FINAL_RESEARCH_REPORT.md), [theorem catalogue](RCPQI_THEOREM_CATALOGUE.md), [negative-results catalogue](RCPQI_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md) and [reproducibility record](RCPQI_REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

Active research and quantum architecture work are suspended. **No V11 is planned.** Reopening requires a precise, cheaply certified structural promise, model-preserving proof, complete cost advantage over equally informed baselines, a credible prior-art gap and independent mathematical review under the [restart protocol](RCPQI_RESTART_PROTOCOL.md).
