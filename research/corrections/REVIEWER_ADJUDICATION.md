# External review adjudication

**Scope:** Post-publication maintenance of the V1–V10 archive. The classifications assess the reviewer observations, not the existence of a new RCP-QI algorithm. Historical raw results are retained. [Original publication README](../../README_AT_PUBLICATION.md).

| Priority | Outcome | Evidence and correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Partial quantum search was omitted | **CONFIRMED** | [Grover–Radhakrishnan](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0407122) and [Korepin](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0503238) predate RCP-QI. The published global/local/final-global sequence identifies a block in `Θ(√N)` generic queries. [Derivation](PARTIAL_SEARCH_PRIOR_ART.md). |
| A. Reported `N=256` thresholds 7/8/9 versus V2 9/10/11 | **CONFIRMED** | Independently enumerated the specified one-shot schedule family with a three-amplitude recurrence, then checked five targets by Qiskit statevectors. These are minimum counts **within that family**; no general finite-size optimality is claimed. [Results](PARTIAL_SEARCH_RESULTS.md). |
| B. V3's “local” reflection was intra-block diffusion | **INCORRECT** as a description of the source; the underlying criticism is **CONFIRMED** | `local_partition_diffuser` calls `_diffuse(circuit, [n - 1])`: only the partition bit. The historical `P_half=1/2` result applies to that circuit and oracle-only control. It does not refute established intra-block diffusion. [Source](../../src/rcp_qi_research/partition_register.py); [tests](../../tests/integration/test_partition_register.py); corrected N06 in the [negative catalogue](../archive/RCPQI_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md). |
| C. Uniqueness needs Valiant–Vazirani context | **CONFIRMED** | A randomized SAT-to-zero/one promise reduction exists; it does not make each unique instance easy or provide a free partition oracle. [Original paper and scope](SAT_AND_COMPLEXITY_PRIOR_ART.md). |
| D. Full Grover is an incomplete quantum 3-SAT baseline | **CONFIRMED** | Ambainis's coherent amplification of structured local search gives the cited `O*(1.153…^n)` bound under its model; quantum backtracking and later algorithms also matter. A certified current best exponent remains **UNRESOLVED** by this focused review. [Source comparison](SAT_AND_COMPLEXITY_PRIOR_ART.md). |
| E. QSETH, relativization, backdoors need careful model separation | **CONFIRMED** | QSETH is conjectural; BBBV is an oracle limitation; backdoor discovery and use are established SAT literature. The claim that every successful explicit-input algorithm “must be non-relativizing” is **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED** only as a proof-barrier warning, not a theorem about every algorithm. [Review](SAT_AND_COMPLEXITY_PRIOR_ART.md). |
| F. Small one-iteration bias is not a general coherent lower bound | **CONFIRMED** | Independent samples cost `Θ(N²)`; additive amplitude estimation uses `O(N)` charged preparation/inverse calls; established partial search uses `Θ(√N)` phase queries. Nonlinear evolution and free postselection are separate models. [Task separation](SAT_AND_COMPLEXITY_PRIOR_ART.md). |
| G. Suggested positive interference examples transfer to worst-case SAT | **INCORRECT** as a general transfer | DQI, Yamakawa–Zhandry and planted noisy kXOR concern distinct optimization, random-oracle or planted promises. Original sources and later limitations are in the [reading guide](FUTURE_READING_GUIDE.md). Their broader relevance is **UNRESOLVED** without a new certified reduction and complete costs. |
| H. Public README was too long and repetitive | **CONFIRMED** | The concise [README](../../README.md) points to the unchanged historical text, corrected V3 scope, code, formalisation, evidence and reproduction. |
| I. Lean theorem count implies mathematical novelty | **INCORRECT** | Lean checks the specific propositions, including elementary identities, modelling lemmas, reductions and restricted soundness. Externally cited complexity bounds are outside this formalisation. [Catalogue](../../THEOREMS.md), [formal status](../../theory/formal_status.md). |

**Claims superseded:** The original V2 note's “local diffuser showed no improvement,” the broad reading of V3/N06 as rejecting local diffusion generally, and any presentation of full-space Grover as the only quantum SAT comparator. The old data and code remain historical controls. No reviewer observation establishes a novel RCP-QI speedup.
