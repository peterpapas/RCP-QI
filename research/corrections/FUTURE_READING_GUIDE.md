# Examples of structural interference, for reading only

These are established or claimed **external** algorithms and are not RCP-QI operators. Their input promises and classical comparators differ from worst-case explicit SAT. No V11 is authorized.

| Work and original source | Problem, structure and mechanism | Cost and transfer boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jordan, Shutty, Wootters, Zalcman, Schmidhuber, King, Isakov and Babbush, *Optimization by Decoded Quantum Interferometry* (arXiv 2024; Nature 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08292) | DQI exploits objective Fourier structure and a coherent coding-theory decoding step. It studies approximation/optimization, including sparse MAX-XORSAT and a polynomial-intersection problem. | Preparation, reversible decoding, Fourier operations, measurement and verification must be counted. The authors report BP-decoded DQI beating simulated annealing on a selected family, **not** specialized classical algorithms tailored to that family; a different polynomial-intersection comparison is presented as an apparent exponential advantage over known polynomial-time classical algorithms. [Later DQI analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14443) and a [structured-instance limitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14509) narrow plausible generalization. These are not worst-case CNF-SAT results. |
| [Yamakawa and Zhandry, *Verifiable Quantum Advantage without Structure* (arXiv 2022; later ACM publication)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02063) | A specially constructed NP search relation, with coherent access to a **random oracle**, has an exponential quantum-versus-classical query separation relative to that oracle. The quantum mechanism uses the relation's coding/hidden structure, not a one-bit SAT verifier for an arbitrary formula. | A candidate witness is classically verifiable, but oracle access is a central resource. The result is relativized/average-case in its stated model; it is neither a generic explicit-SAT algorithm nor an NP-completeness result. Any SAT transfer must build the promised oracle at charged cost. |
| [Schmidhuber, O'Donnell, Kothari and Babbush, *Quartic quantum speedups for planted inference* (SODA 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19378) | Planted noisy `k`XOR/sparse LPN has a statistically planted signal; a Kikuchi-method sparse-Hamiltonian formulation supports quantum spectral inference. The original comparison gave a near-quartic runtime gain against then-known classical algorithms in its parameter regime, with few qubits relative to the classical space. | Hamiltonian access/construction, sparse data queries, precision, output and success amplification are part of the model. [Gupta, He, O'Donnell and Singer's 2025 classical improvement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.09422) reduces the claimed gap to about quadratic for large constant `k` (while a space advantage may remain). This is a planted inference promise, not worst-case SAT. |

Before adapting any example, specify an explicit input family, recognition/certification cost, the exact coherent oracle, state preparation, decoding or spectral cost, output verification and an equally informed classical baseline. The [restart protocol](../archive/RCPQI_RESTART_PROTOCOL.md) still applies.
