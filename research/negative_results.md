# Negative results and inconclusive outcomes

**Archive status: SUSPENDED — PENDING NEW MATHEMATICAL INSIGHT.** The final [negative-results catalogue](archive/RCPQI_NEGATIVE_RESULTS.md) adds the original rationale, evidence and mandatory reopening condition for each mechanism. This historical log is retained unchanged below.

1. **V1 one-iteration bias vanishes asymptotically.** The analytic correct-half advantage is (4/N-8/N^2). Repeated independent partition-bit sampling at one iteration needs order (N^2) shots for fixed confidence. See `results/one_iteration.json`.
2. **V2 full-space Grover does not give cheap partition existence.** Correct-half probability is algebraically linked to exact-solution probability; fixed-accuracy detection still takes order (\sqrt N) phase queries. The plotted small cases support this relationship, not a speedup.
3. **V3 partition-bit-only diffusion fails in tested cases.** It leaves correct-half probability at `0.5`. This reflection acts on the partition bit only; it is not intra-block diffusion. Established [partial quantum search](corrections/PARTIAL_SEARCH_PRIOR_ART.md) combines intra-block and global operations and improves constant factors for block identification, while retaining `Θ(√N)` generic queries. An ancilla copied before data-only Grover has zero left/right trace distance; copied after Grover it exposes no more than the existing data bias. Historical evidence: `results/phase2_partition_register.json`; separate [corrected experiment](corrections/PARTIAL_SEARCH_RESULTS.md).
4. **V4 clause-parity phase is not a SAT conjunction oracle.** It phases odd counts of violated clauses. Occasional satisfying measurements do not make it a correct replacement. Unit propagation already solves the simplest unique instance without quantum work.
5. **V4 zero-solution quantum runs can be inconclusive.** Exhausting a finite measurement budget does not certify UNSAT. The saved zero-no-units quantum runs remain inconclusive; DPLL proves the instance unsatisfiable.
6. **V6 recursive confidence costs exceed full-space Grover in the saved tiny groups.** The benchmark retains one failed first run and all restart costs. The sample is too small for a general performance ratio.
7. **V5 structure-aware branching is classical and does not beat direct Glucose4 in these small controls.** Some heuristics reduce internal decisions relative to prefix self-reduction on certain seeds, but direct PySAT is usually cheaper and lookahead/symmetry scoring adds classical work. No quantum operator was run, so no quantum advantage is inferred. See `results/phase2_structural.json`.
8. **V5.1 finds a local branch-choice gain but no direct-solver crossover.** On three completed satisfiable 256-variable random 3-SAT formulas, occurrence's median time was 0.38 times prefix's, yet 5.06 times direct Glucose4's. Across 95 paired SAT cases, occurrence lost to prefix by over 5 ms on 40 and won on 3. At 512-variable random 3-SAT, no recursive method completed under two seconds, while direct Glucose4 completed 1/6 and CaDiCaL 2/6. This censored, two-seed study cannot rule out a future crossover. See `research/v5_1_findings.md`.
9. **V5.1 structural preprocessing can dominate tractable instances.** Symmetry scoring and some occurrence runs time out at 1,024–2,048 variables while direct CDCL and dedicated XOR/2-SAT methods finish. Pigeonhole UNSAT timed out at nominal 128–512 for all compared methods; branch heuristics have no opportunity to help before the root verdict. The public UNSAT case has no independently checked certificate.
10. **V7 supplied total complementarity is classically sufficient.** One verifier value and one transformation application produce a witness. An involutive total complement cannot coexist with a unique solution on a space larger than two. These are conditional mathematical findings, not measured runtime comparisons.
11. **V7 generic quotient search is still generic search.** A known quotient/section preserves the existence answer and transports one verifier query in either direction. If the quotient verifier has no further structure, its quantum query complexity remains that of unstructured search over quotient labels. This does not rule out structured quotients.
12. **V7 one naive Walsh sample does not locate a unique target.** The `H–U_f–H` measurement distribution is independent of target position. Other coherent procedures are not excluded. Semantic certification of even a supplied validity-preserving bit flip can encode UNSAT, so treating such a certificate as free can hide the hard problem. See `research/v7_mathematical_review.md`.
13. **V7.1 local complementarity has no automatic quantum gain.** An efficiently accessible point in a certified complementing domain yields a witness after one classical verifier value. Flipping one constraint while preserving an arbitrary residual only converts a *given* residual witness; `F(x,b)=b∧H(x)` is satisfiable exactly when arbitrary `H` is. This is a scoped Lean-backed negative result, not an impossibility theorem for all structural quantum methods.
14. **V7.1 algebraic syntax is insufficient.** An affine subsystem can be eliminated classically while leaving arbitrary 3-SAT on free variables. General quadratic systems over GF(2) are NP-complete; a polynomial-time clause-to-quadratic encoding does not solve them. Bounded-width constraint hypergraphs admit established classical dynamic programming, so a quantum claim needs a proved additional advantage. No V8 operator passed the gate.
15. **V7.1 established oracle separations do not imply SAT speedups.** Simon requires a many-output two-to-one XOR oracle, Shor requires modular periodicity, and hidden-shift algorithms require a shifted-function promise. A one-bit SAT verifier does not supply these structures. The relevant oracle-construction reductions remain open.
16. **V8 arithmetic-to-Boolean transfer is invalid without a new theorem.** The integer expression `z²−z` is zero at both Boolean inputs but nonzero at `2` (Lean). Formal polynomial lower bounds can depend on coefficients and derivatives that Boolean-equivalent expressions change. This refutes an unqualified inference from the new VP–VNP claims to SAT/quantum lower bounds; it does not refute those arithmetic claims.
17. **V8 compact algebraic representation is not cheap partition extraction.** A polynomial-size CNF validity circuit evaluates one assignment, while the partition count still sums over assignments. In characteristic two, two witnesses can cancel. Low-width and affine promises have strong classical baselines; no costed V8 quantum-specific operator passed the gate. See `research/v8/V8_MATHEMATICAL_TRANSFER.md`.
18. **V9 symbolic-output hardness does not imply hard partition decisions.** A polynomial-size exactly-one CNF for permutation matrices has permanent as its full assignment-weight generating polynomial, requiring `m!` expanded terms. Every fixed-edge partition existence question reduces to polynomial-time bipartite matching. A compressed-DAG lower bound remains conditional on an accepted arithmetic result outside characteristic two.
19. **V9 raw algebraic measures are not Boolean invariants.** `(z²−z)∏(1+a_i b_i)` equals zero on the Boolean cube yet has raw coefficient flattening rank `2^k` and a small arithmetic DAG. Canonical multilinear measures avoid this representation ambiguity, but computing or representing them still incurs costs. See `research/v9/V9_BOOLEAN_IDEAL_ANALYSIS.md`.
20. **V9 black-box half-label extraction retains search-scale queries.** Under a unique-marker membership oracle and bounded error, the balanced-half bit needs `Θ(√N)` quantum queries by a cross-half adversary matrix. This says nothing about unrestricted explicit-CNF SAT gate complexity or structured promises. See `research/v9/V9_QUANTUM_TRANSFER.md`.
21. **V10 distributed SAT communication does not lower-bound central SAT time.** Positive unit clauses held by Alice and negative units held by Bob encode set disjointness, inheriting its communication lower bounds. Once the union is centrally available, contradictory units are detected by a linear scan. A width-one equality CNF has exponential evaluation-matrix rank but trivial SAT existence.
22. **V10 branch-discard proof bounds require checkable leaves.** Original-clause conflict trees translate to tree-like resolution with weakening and inherit its lower bounds on the same unsatisfiable formulas. Opaque SAT/quantum partition verdicts, CDCL learning and stronger proof systems do not satisfy that certificate model without a new simulation. See `research/v10/V10_PROOF_COMPLEXITY.md`.
23. **V10 novelty gate did not pass.** The two selected theorem candidates reduce directly to known disjointness and DPLL/resolution frameworks. A Boolean-invariant measure being well-defined did not make it cheaply obtainable from an explicit CNF. No general impossibility or quantum advantage is inferred.

These findings remain part of the project history. A new operator should be compared against them at matched success targets and full resource cost.
